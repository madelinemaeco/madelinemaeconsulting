Systems & Setup

I work in your systems — or help you find the right one.

If you already have a property management platform in place, I will work directly within it — no disruption, no learning curve on your end. If you are not using one yet or are open to a better fit, I will assess your portfolio and recommend the platform that best suits your needs. Either way, before anything else, I conduct a full system audit to make sure everything is accurate and set up correctly going forward.

  • ✓Work within your existing platform or recommend one that fits your portfolio
  • ✓Review all property and unit records for accuracy and completeness
  • ✓Audit all tenant ledgers — correct any misposted charges, credits, or balances
  • ✓Verify lease dates, rent amounts, and terms against actual agreements
  • ✓Review owner settings, fee configurations, and disbursement schedules
  • ✓Confirm vendors, recurring charges, and automated transactions are correct
  • ✓Identify missing documentation or accounts needing cleanup
  • ✓Deliver a summary report of all findings and corrections made
